Plan

Content strategy & planning

We start with your business, not a shot list: who you want to reach, what you want them to do and what your current content is and isn't doing.

  • Content audit of your current channels
  • Audience, competitor and goal review
  • Your pillar mix and content priorities
  • The measures we'll report on each month

Brand storytelling

We find the stories that make your business yours and turn them into messages and recurring formats your audience will recognize.

  • Story discovery session with owners and team
  • Key messages and brand voice
  • Recurring story formats and series

Content calendar & campaigns

A month-by-month plan that ties content to what's happening in the business: menu changes, seasons, events and slow days.

  • Monthly content calendar you approve
  • Seasonal and menu-launch campaigns
  • Event, catering and holiday pushes

How we work together

Monthly
partnerships

Content works when it's consistent. Our clients work with us month to month: a plan, the production to carry it out, and a review of what it achieved.

Every month includes

  • A planning session and an approved content calendar
  • Shoot days scheduled around your service hours
  • Edited photos, videos and posts, published on schedule
  • A performance report and recommendations

Getting started

Every partnership starts with a strategy session and a review of your current content, so the first month is built on your goals, not guesses. Launches and events can also be planned as stand-alone campaigns.
