Our approach

Five pillars. One strategy.

Great restaurant content does five different jobs. We plan every month around the right balance of them, based on your goals and what the numbers tell us.

AppetiteAuthorityExperienceConnectionConversionPartnership
01
Pillar 01

Appetite

Make people crave it.

The first job of restaurant content is simple: make someone hungry enough to stop scrolling. We plan and shoot the dishes, textures and moments that sell the plate before anyone reads the menu.

What it looks like

  • Hero dish photography
  • Sizzle, pour and plating moments
  • Menu and new-item launches

Supports Discovery, cravings and orders of the items that matter most to your margins.

02
Pillar 02

Authority

Show why you're the real thing.

Guests choose places they trust. Authority content shows the standards behind the food: ingredients, technique, consistency and a visual identity people recognize at a glance.

What it looks like

  • Ingredients, sourcing and technique
  • Signature dishes and house methods
  • A consistent look across every channel

Supports Trust, premium perception and standing out from the place down the street.

03
Pillar 03

Experience

Sell the visit, not just the plate.

People don't only buy food; they choose an evening, a lunch break, a celebration. Experience content shows the room, the energy and the service so guests can picture themselves there.

What it looks like

  • Atmosphere, interiors and the room at its best
  • Service, hospitality and the team at work
  • Events, private dining and occasions

Supports Reservations, group bookings and being the obvious choice for an occasion.

04
Pillar 04

Connection

Introduce the people behind it.

The story behind a restaurant is something no competitor can copy. Connection content introduces the owners, the cooks, the family recipes and the regulars, and gives people a reason to care.

What it looks like

  • Founder, family and chef stories
  • Behind-the-scenes and the team's personalities
  • Community, regulars and local partners

Supports Loyalty, word of mouth and followers who feel like regulars.

05
Pillar 05

Conversion

Turn attention into visits.

Awareness only matters if it leads somewhere. Conversion content gives people a clear reason and a clear next step, and we track how it performs so the next campaign is sharper than the last.

What it looks like

  • Limited-time items, launches and seasonal campaigns
  • Event, catering and private-dining promotion
  • Clear calls to reserve, order or visit

Supports Inquiries, reservations, orders and visits you can track.
